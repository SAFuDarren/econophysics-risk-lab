# Frozen statistical design

The unit of analysis is one nonoverlapping target calendar month. There are 81 evaluation units, January 2020–September 2026. Each paired comparison uses the exact same months. No observations, factors, origins or models were selected by evaluation performance. Months are the paired analysis units; daily observations are not treated as independent replications.

## Six primary contrasts

1. FullCov−Diagonal mean QLIKE.
2. FullCov−HAR-All mean QLIKE.
3. Performance−Fixed next-month daily second moment.
4. Performance−Fixed held coefficient HHI.
5. Performance−Permuted next-month daily second moment.
6. Performance−Permuted held coefficient HHI.

HAR-Portfolio evaluation loss is reported descriptively as a pre-specified benchmark. No seventh significance comparison was added after its descriptive performance was observed. Correlation-mode paths and signed-covariance ratios are descriptive estimates; they are not inferred counts of independently sampled investors.

For each locked contrast, 4,999 circular-block bootstrap replicates jointly resample the paired month differences. Three consecutive months form the primary block; six months form a frozen sensitivity analysis. A common deterministic seed, 11062026, is used at each block size. The 95% basic interval is `[2*mean−q.975(bootstrapmean), 2*mean−q.025(bootstrapmean)]`. The two-sided p value resamples centered paired differences and uses the add-one rule `(1+count(|nullmean|>=|observedmean|))/5000`. Holm adjustment applies jointly to the six contrasts, separately for each block size. These intervals are unadjusted intervals; statistical claims follow the corrected p values.

Paired SD and paired standardized effect `mean/SD` are saved. Relative effect is the paired mean divided by the reference mean, not the mean of per-month ratios. QLIKE is `y/p−log(y/p)−1`; daily second-moment targets and all forecasts are positive. The HAR fitting objective matches this positive Gamma log-mean criterion plus ridge. Standardization parameters are learned on fit months only. Development selection considers only the three frozen penalties for each representation; fitting is not repeated on fit plus development. Iterated Gamma fitting must pass a normalized gradient threshold of 1e−5.

## Inferential boundary

Calendar blocks address short-run dependence under a historical stationarity approximation; they do not provide robustness to all structural breaks. The difference between three- and six-month HHI p values is reported. Current-vintage retrospective data are not a point-in-time or prospective deployment test. A permutation of six score labels at each month is a named control path, not a randomization test of observed institutional behavior, and no claims are based on selecting a favorable permutation.

Forecast methods receive the same six raw series with 252 observations at each origin, and identical fit/development dates. HAR-Portfolio compresses this input into three features, whereas HAR-All uses 42 features. This matches input availability rather than parameter count. The allocation comparison has an additional persistent computed budget state generated by its fixed past updates; it is not claimed to be a 252-day information-budget comparison between competing prediction models.

All risk outcomes are gross. The descriptive 1 bp coefficient-turnover charge does not estimate within-factor trading costs. No wealth process, economic utility gain or statistically established return-timing advantage is inferred.

Complete numbers are in `tables/primary_contrasts.csv` and `results_summary.json`.
