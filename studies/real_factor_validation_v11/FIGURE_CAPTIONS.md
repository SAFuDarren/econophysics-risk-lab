# Figure RF1 | Nominal factor diversity, signed covariance and historical risk forecasts

**a**, Effective correlation modes from the previous 63 daily observations of all six official research factors, sampled at each month-end origin. The dashed line is the nominal factor count of six. **b**, Full-covariance versus diagonal second-moment estimates for the same inverse-volatility coefficient vector. The diagonal estimate retains identical means and marginal variances; only cross-covariances are removed. Ratios below one indicate net covariance hedging. Panels a and b display estimated historical paths, not replicate averages; no sampling bands are implied. **c**, Mean QLIKE of all four frozen forecast methods over the complete 81-month evaluation. This panel reports descriptive averages, with the locked paired comparisons in d. HAR-Portfolio has the lowest mean loss. **d**, FullCov minus each pre-specified reference, with 95% basic paired circular-block bootstrap intervals (4,999 replicates). Circles use three-month blocks; triangles show the frozen six-month sensitivity. Negative differences favor FullCov. The six-comparison Holm-adjusted p values for FullCov−Diagonal and FullCov−HAR-All are 0.2562 and 0.0810 with three-month blocks, and 0.3088 and 0.0850 with six-month blocks; neither establishes a corrected prediction advantage.

All panels use January 2020–September 2026 (n = 81 nonoverlapping target calendar months). The source panel comprises MktRF, SMB, HML, RMW, CMA and Mom daily arithmetic research-factor returns, with prior-252-day information for coefficients and HAR features. Training targets are in 1991–2009; penalty selection is in 2010–2019. The current data vintage was downloaded after protocol/code freeze, and predictions were hashed before evaluation truth was joined. This is a historical current-vintage study, not a point-in-time or prospective trading test. Source data: `tables/predictions_scored.csv`, `tables/forecast_scores.csv`, `tables/primary_contrasts.csv`. Original source: Kenneth R. French Data Library. Return correlations do not identify trading-action correlations.

# Figure RF2 | Allocation concentration and subsequent risk are distinct outcomes

**a**, Held coefficient HHI for the fixed equal-risk-budget rule, the lagged-performance update and a date-seeded score-permuted update. Each budget is converted to held coefficients using the preceding 252-day factor volatility; coefficients are fixed for the next month. These are computed allocation paths applied to observed research-factor returns, not observed fund flows. No seed-average or sampling uncertainty is represented by the paths. **b**, Paired mean HHI differences, Performance minus each reference. **c**, Paired mean next-month daily second-moment differences for the same two comparisons; the plotting unit is 10⁻⁶ in squared decimal arithmetic returns. Both risk intervals include zero. Panels b and c use 95% basic paired circular-block bootstrap intervals, 4,999 replicates; circles use three-month blocks and triangles use six-month blocks. The same 81 evaluation months enter all comparisons.

Performance−Fixed HHI is 0.01736 (three-month interval 0.00701–0.02713; Holm-adjusted p = 0.0036), with six-month adjusted p = 0.0540. The score-permuted control has higher mean HHI than Performance; Performance−Permuted adjusted p = 0.1624. Mean next-month risk is 34.8% higher for Performance than Fixed, but that increment is not established (three-month adjusted p = 0.2562), nor is the 21.5% increment against Permuted (adjusted p = 0.4844). Holm correction is over the same six frozen primary comparisons. Coefficients sum to one at the factor-sleeve level and do not specify underlying gross exposure or a funded NAV. Source data: `tables/computed_allocations.csv`, `tables/predictions_scored.csv`, `tables/primary_contrasts.csv`. Original source: Kenneth R. French Data Library.
