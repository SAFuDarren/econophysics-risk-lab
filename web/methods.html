Shared actions become squared-size risk共同动作形成规模平方风险

An ER graph H has N traders and independent edge probability p. The expected mean-degree parameter c is the original project parameter ρ. It is an ensemble expectation; the realized mean degree of one graph can fluctuate.ER图H含N名交易者，每条边以独立概率p存在。期望平均度参数c对应原项目参数ρ；它是总体期望，单张图的实际平均度可以涨落。

Every connected group C, with size sC, shares one action ξC. Sell, inactivity and buy have probabilities q/2, 1−q and q/2. Different groups choose independently. The code uses a = q/2.每个大小为sC的连通群体共享一个动作ξC，卖出、不交易和买入概率分别为q/2、1−q、q/2。不同群体独立选择。代码使用a=q/2。

With full execution against an outside counterparty, signed orders change log price while absolute activity defines volume. λ is the price response and u is unit order size.在外部对手方全部成交的约定下，带符号的订单改变对数价格，绝对活动量定义成交量。λ是价格响应，u是单位订单量。

A four-node chain and a four-node star both form one group of four. Their possible observations are (−λ,4u), (0,0) and (+λ,4u), with the same respective probabilities q/2, 1−q and q/2.四节点链和四节点星均形成一个大小为4的群体。可能观测为(−λ,4u)、(0,0)、(+λ,4u)，对应概率同为q/2、1−q、q/2。

A necessary restriction from shared activity共同活动给出的必要约束

Centering removes independent-group products. A same-group contribution carries sC² × sC = sC³. Normalizing by the earlier variance relations gives A and B.中心化消去独立群体的乘积项。同一群体的贡献带有sC²×sC=sC³。用前面的方差关系归一化，得到A和B。

Since 1 ≤ sC ≤ N, sC² ≤ sC³ ≤ NsC². The bound applies to any graph distribution under the fixed action and observation assumptions. κ₃(V) is the third central moment. Market calculations center returns before forming r².由1≤sC≤N，有sC²≤sC³≤NsC²。该界对固定动作与观测假设下的任意网络分布成立。κ₃(V)为三阶中心矩；市场计算先对收益中心化，再形成r²。

A separate historical forecasting task独立的历史预测任务

A complete UTC day contains 288 five-minute returns. The target is next-day log realized variance.一个完整UTC日含288个五分钟收益；预测目标是下一日的对数实现方差。

The three HAR inputs use today, the past seven days and the past 28 days. The full model adds two activity, three price–volume coupling and two concentration features, for ten inputs.三个HAR输入分别使用当日、过去7日和过去28日。完整模型另加两项活动、三项量价联合和两项集中度特征，共10项。

Standardization and weights use fitting dates. Training uses 337 targets in 2025; validation uses 90 in 2026 Q1. One refit uses all 427 earlier targets. Coefficients are then frozen for the 183 April–September test dates, while observed history updates.标准化与权重只使用拟合日期。2025年337个目标用于训练，2026年第一季度90个用于选参；合并427个早期目标重拟合一次。随后在4至9月183个测试日期固定系数，同时更新已观测历史输入。

The iid fixed-parameter mechanism has constant next-period conditional second moment. The HAR application uses observed market memory; it is evaluated as a separate empirical model.独立同分布、固定参数的机制模型具有恒定的下一期条件二阶矩。HAR应用使用观测到的市场记忆，作为独立实证模型评价。
