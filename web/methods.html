Translate the model into actions先把符号翻成交易动作

N counts traders, u is the order size per active trader, and q is the chance a group trades. One group action ξ can be buy (+1), sell (−1) or inactive (0). In the symmetric model the buy and sell probabilities are both q/2. The price scale λ converts signed action to a return. E is an average weighted by probability, ∑ adds terms, and conditioning on H means holding the graph fixed.N 是交易者人数，u 是每名活跃交易者的订单大小，q 是一个群体采取交易动作的概率。共同动作 ξ 可取 +1（买）、−1（卖）或 0（不动）。对称模型中买与卖各占 q/2。λ 把带方向的动作量换成收益率。E 表示按概率取平均，∑ 表示加总，竖线后写 H 表示先固定网络。

A graph draws traders as dots and their connections as lines. The ER graph samples links independently. A connected component is a group reachable along those lines. Here its s people share one action, so multiplying that action by s multiplies its variance by s².图把交易者画成点、关系画成线。ER 随机图独立抽取各条连线，沿线可互相到达的点构成一个连通群体。这里 s 人共享同一个动作，所以动作乘以 s，方差乘以 s²。

With full execution against an outside counterparty, signed orders change log price while absolute activity defines volume. λ is the price response and u is unit order size.在外部对手方全部成交的约定下，带符号的订单改变对数价格，绝对活动量定义成交量。λ是价格响应，u是单位订单量。
