Start with two traders从两名交易者开始

Keep the chance of trading fixed. Change only whether the two traders decide together.固定每人的交易概率，只改变两个人是否一起做决定。

  1. Write down one round记录一轮交易

    Buy one unit, sell one unit or sit out. Call the signed choice X. With one buyer and one seller, net demand is zero but two units change hands.买 1 单位、卖 1 单位，或不交易。用带符号的 X 记录选择。一买一卖时，净需求为零，成交量为 2。

    A return measures the price change relative to the starting price.收益率用起始价格衡量价格变了多少。

  2. Take an average across rounds对多轮取平均

    Buy or sell with 10% probability each and sit out with 80%. An expected value weights each outcome by its probability.买入和卖出各有 10% 的概率，其余 80% 不交易。期望按概率加权。

  3. Measure the spread算波动有多大

    Variance averages the squared distance from the mean. Here the mean signed trade is zero.方差是结果离均值的距离平方后取平均。这里带符号的交易量均值为零。

  4. Compare the two ways of trading比较两种交易方式

    When the decisions are independent, add the two variances. With a shared decision, net demand is +2 or −2 with 10% probability each.各自独立决定时，把两人的方差相加。一起决定时，净需求有 10% 的概率为 +2，10% 为 −2。

    Independent decisions各自决定
    Shared decision一起决定

    Average volume is 0.4 units either way. The cross term explains the difference in spread.两种情况的平均成交量都是 0.4。起伏不同，来自平方展开后的交叉项。

    Covariance measures how deviations from the two means move together. Here the means are zero, so the last term averages to zero for independent choices. With a shared choice it is positive each time they trade.协方差衡量两项偏离各自均值时是否一起变化。这里均值为零，各自决定时最后一项平均为零。一起决定时，只要交易，最后一项就是正的。

The starting model multiplies net demand F by a fixed price response k. Variance is multiplied by its square, so the ratio between the two cases stays the same.原模型把净需求 F 乘以固定的价格响应 k。方差乘上响应的平方，两种情况之间的风险倍数因此保留。

Translate the model into actions先把符号翻成交易动作

Each group makes one choice. Holding H fixed means we keep the same links while repeating the trades.每个群组只作一次共同决定。固定 H，就是用同一张网络图重复观察交易。

A network links traders. In an Erdős–Rényi (ER) graph, each possible link is drawn independently. Traders joined by paths of links form one group.网络把交易者连起来。在 Erdős–Rényi（ER）随机图中，每条可能的边独立生成。能沿边相互到达的人构成一个群组。

Conditional moments and hidden states条件矩与隐藏状态Random graphs and percolation随机图与渗流Generating functions and group moments生成函数与群体矩

An ER graph H has N traders and independent edge probability p. The expected mean-degree parameter c is the original project parameter ρ. It is an ensemble expectation; the realized mean degree of one graph can fluctuate.ER图H含N名交易者，每条边以独立概率p存在。期望平均度参数c对应原项目参数ρ；它是总体期望，单张图的实际平均度可以涨落。

Every connected group C, with size sC, shares one action ξC. Sell, inactivity and buy have probabilities q/2, 1−q and q/2. Different groups choose independently. The code uses a = q/2.每个大小为sC的连通群体共享一个动作ξC，卖出、不交易和买入概率分别为q/2、1−q、q/2。不同群体独立选择。代码使用a=q/2。

With full execution against an outside counterparty, signed orders change log price while absolute activity defines volume. λ is the price response and u is unit order size.在外部对手方全部成交的约定下，带符号的订单改变对数价格，绝对活动量定义成交量。λ是价格响应，u是单位订单量。

For every graph, the conditional means are E[r|H] = 0 and E[V|H] = quN. Their between-graph variance is zero.对每张图，条件均值均为E[r|H]=0、E[V|H]=quN，因此条件均值在图之间的方差为零。

Population moments identify selected ER parameters总体矩识别指定ER参数

Reverse the model calculation把模型计算倒过来

In the forward calculation, q, size u and the graph determine moments of returns and volume. In inversion we start from measured moments. Fixing N and u lets average volume determine q, while volume variance constrains the expected squared group sizes.正向计算是给定活动率 q、订单大小 u 和网络，推出收益与成交量的统计量。反演从统计量倒过来。固定 N 和 u 后，平均成交量给出 q，成交量方差约束群体规模平方和的期望值。

Example. In the model, N = 100, u = 1 and mean volume 20 give q = 20/100 = 0.2. An inference changes if those known inputs or model assumptions change.例子。模型里 N = 100、u = 1，平均成交量为 20，就有 q = 20/100 = 0.2。改变已知量或假设，倒推的解释也会变。

Assume known N ≥ 2 and u > 0, fixed 0 < q < 1 and λ > 0, with independent ER redraws. The forward relations can then be inverted.已知N≥2、u>0，固定0<q<1、λ>0，并独立重采样ER图。此时可以反演正向矩关系。

Adding an edge inside a group leaves S₂ unchanged. Joining groups of sizes a and b changes S₂ by the following positive amount.在群体内部加边不改变S₂。连接大小为a和b的两个群体，会使S₂增加下列正值。

A third central moment averages cubed deviations, so extreme positive and negative observations contribute with opposite signs. The joint term here measures how squared return and volume vary together.三阶中心矩把偏离均值的距离立方后平均，极大的正偏差与负偏差符号相反。这里的联合项衡量收益平方怎样与成交量一起变化。

The fixed model predicts a positive Δ. The comparison asks whether observed moments fall in that range, which can motivate a change to the observation assumptions.固定模型要求 Δ 为正。实际计算检查观测值是否落在该范围，结果用来判断观测假设需不需要调整。

Centering removes independent-group products. A same-group contribution carries sC² × sC = sC³. Normalizing by the earlier variance relations gives the diagnostic ratios AΔ and BΔ.中心化消去独立群体的乘积项。同一群体的贡献带有sC²×sC=sC³。用前面的方差关系归一化，得到诊断比值AΔ与BΔ。

Temporal order adds a separate kind of information时间顺序增加另一类信息

Allow the market to stay active for a while让活跃程度随时间延续

The time subscript t marks a window. If activity probability is high today, it can remain high in the next window. The law of total variance separates variation within an activity state from variation in the mean across states.下标 t 表示一个时间窗。一个时间窗很活跃，下个时间窗也可能继续活跃。全方差公式把总波动拆成状态内部的波动，以及状态改变造成的平均值波动。

Example. Even if volume is always 10 in calm windows and 30 in busy windows, mixing the two states gives positive variance.例子。即使平静窗口的成交量总是 10、活跃窗口总是 30，把两种窗口合起来，成交量仍会有方差。

Weighted decisions, interdependence and changing alignment有效交易权重、相互依赖与群体转向

Replace head counts with trading weights从人数推广到交易权重

Q is the amount represented by an action. Two small traders and one large trader can exert different pressure, even with the same number of decisions. Covariance records whether actions align across groups or across time.Q 是一个动作代表的交易量。决策数量相同，订单大小不同，压力就可能不同。协方差记录群体之间、时间窗之间的动作是否同向。

A fast layer can respond quickly and a slow layer retains memory. The tanh function turns a signed input into a bounded response. The model uses it to give stronger aligned input a larger probability of a positive action.快层响应较快，慢层保留较久记忆。tanh 把输入映射到有上下限的区间。这里它用来让较强的正向输入对应更高的正向动作概率。

Replace equal counts with effective trading weights将等量人数推广为有效交易权重

Under independent symmetric group actions, Var(ξC)=q and the cross terms vanish, recovering q times the squared-weight sum. Coupling can add positive or negative covariance. Effective weights describe directional trading scale, not identified numbers of people.群体动作独立、对称且Var(ξC)=q时，交叉项消失，回到q乘权重平方和。引入依赖后，协方差可正可负。有效权重描述同向交易规模，不直接对应实际人数。

Each node carries a positive trading weight and a direction in each layer. In the environment experiments, ρc is residual-innovation correlation across agents, κ feeds the last actual normalized calendar return into decisions, and L is normalized latent accommodation. These definitions are separate from the ER network mean-degree parameter c. The predictable-impact subtraction χ belongs to the price equation.环境实验中的 ρc 是跨主体残差创新相关；κ 把上一根实际归一化日历收益反馈到决策；L 是归一化隐藏承接容量。它们与 ER 网络平均度参数 c 分开。可预期影响扣除 χ 属于价格方程。

Write return as the predicted part μ plus price noise. Conditional on the current state, the noise has mean zero and unit variance before scaling.把收益写成可预期部分 μ 加价格噪声。给定当前状态，噪声在缩放前的均值为零、方差为一。

Conditional integration adds these squared-return terms. Actual random returns still update the simulated state. The predictable component includes drift and transmitted pressure. Identifying actual trader groups would require additional evidence.条件积分把这些收益平方项加起来。模拟状态仍由实际生成的随机收益更新。可预期部分包括漂移和传导到价格的交易压力。要识别真实交易群体，还需要其他证据。

A causal mean contrast and forecast improvement are different targets. An effective state must contain variation not already encoded by the matched-information comparator, and that variation must be recoverable from the permitted past observations. The new diagnostics isolate these two requirements.因果平均差值与预测改善是不同目标。有效状态必须包含同信息对照尚未吸收的差异，并且这些差异能从允许的历史观测中恢复。新增诊断分开检验这两个要求。

Infer effective directional and temporal structure推断有效方向结构与时间结构

Mean flow, size, direction and persistence enter the risk estimate. Linking G to actual groups would require knowing how individual participants generate executions.风险估计还考虑平均成交流、成交大小和持续性。要把 G 对应到真实群组，还需要知道不同参与者怎样产生这些成交。

Using the same window just recovers flow variance. A forecast adds a testable assumption: trade sizes can change faster than directional amplification.同一窗口相乘，只是把成交流方差算回来。要做预测，再加入一个可检验的假设：成交大小比方向放大变化得快。

The three terms are squared mean return, price variation from flow, and variation left over after accounting for flow.右边三项分别是平均收益的平方、成交流传导到价格的波动，以及成交流解释之后剩下的波动。

The replay fits the response on 30 completed minutes before play. It keeps that fit fixed, using 3 minutes for size and 15 minutes for directional amplification.回放用开局前 30 个完整分钟拟合价格响应，局中固定。成交大小取过去 3 分钟，方向放大取过去 15 分钟。

From observable histories to joint future risk从可见历史到联合未来风险

Keep several plausible states保留多种可能的状态

I is the history we can see. S represents an unobserved state. P(S given I) gives weights to the states compatible with that history. It can stay spread out even when we have many observations.I 是能看到的历史，S 是看不到的状态。给定 I 时的 P(S) 给与历史相容的状态分配权重。观察很多之后，仍可能有多种合理的状态。

For each plausible state, propagate a complete path, add the squared returns within that path and average over paths. Squaring the average return would discard variation between paths.从每个可能状态传播一条完整路径，先把该路径中的收益平方相加，再对路径取加权平均。如果先平均收益再平方，路径之间的差异就会被丢掉。

J is the number of complete scenarios and w their common weights. The target is future realized variance in squared log-return units. Cumulative-return variance is evaluated separately because it also includes cross-time covariance.J为完整情景数，w为共同权重；目标是未来实现方差，单位为对数收益平方。流、收益、响应和残差矩均来自同一批情景，保留联合协方差相容性。累计收益方差另行评价，因为它还包含跨时间协方差。

Portfolio variance retains cross-asset terms组合方差保留跨资产项

Add the assets, then square先加权合并，再求组合方差

Each weight w gives an asset’s share of the portfolio return. The covariance matrix Σ has each asset’s variance on the diagonal and co-movement between assets off the diagonal. Positive covariance can add risk for long positions. Negative covariance can offset it.w 是资产在组合中的权重。协方差矩阵 Σ 的对角线上是各资产的方差，其余格子是两两共同波动。持有同方向仓位时，正协方差会加风险，负协方差可以抵消。

Example. With weights of 1/2, two returns of +1% and −1% sum to zero for that period. The diagonal comparison suppresses cross-asset terms to measure what those terms contribute in the same sample.例子。各占一半时，+1% 和 −1% 在这一期抵消。对角对照把跨资产项设为零，查看它们在同一批数据中对风险估计贡献了多少。
