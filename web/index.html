Herding, price–volume inference and risk羊群机制与量价推断：
市场风险的建模与检验

We study how macro market observations reveal effective collective trading structure, and how that structure enters risk estimates.我们研究宏观市场观测能够揭示哪些有效集体交易结构，以及这些结构如何进入风险估计。

Methods combine exact moments, finite random graphs and controlled simulations with Bitcoin and sector-ETF tests.方法结合精确矩关系、有限随机图与受控模拟，并用比特币和行业ETF数据检验。

For known population and order size, price–volume moments recover finite-ER parameters; distinct connected graphs can share the same observation law.在人数与单位订单规模已知时，量价矩可以恢复有限ER参数；不同连通图仍可能共享相同的完整观测分布。

From market observations to statistical structure and risk从宏观观测量，到统计结构，再到风险

We develop and test a mechanism-based route from a small observable market description to interpretable risk estimates.我们构建并检验一条机制驱动的路线：从少量可观测市场信息，得到可解释的风险估计。

  1. Macro observations宏观观测量

    Start with price returns, volume, counts and signed aggressor flow—the market quantities available at each time.从每个时点可获得的收益、成交量、笔数与主动买卖成交流出发。

    Define the observable inputs明确可观测输入

  2. Statistical structure统计结构推断

    Use moment relations to estimate activity, size concentration, directional amplification and persistence under explicit model assumptions.通过矩关系，在明确模型假设下估计活动、大小集中度、方向放大与持续性。

    Connect observables to effective structure连接观测量与有效结构

  3. Risk mapping风险映射与预测

    Map flow means and dispersion through price response and residual risk; dynamic extensions carry the estimated structure forward.通过价格响应和残差风险，将成交流均值与离散程度映射到风险，并用动态扩展描述其后续演化。

    From estimated moments to risk从估计的矩到风险

  4. Evaluation and use检验与应用

    Evaluate forecasts at matched input budgets and inspect the mechanism through real-market replay and shared-risk diagnostics.按相同输入预算检验预测，并用真实回放和共同风险诊断解释机制与应用。

The microscopic model explains why these aggregate moments contain information about collective trading. Assumption diagnostics guide extensions of the measurement and dynamic model; the central task remains inference and risk prediction.微观模型解释这些聚合矩为何包含集体交易的信息。假设诊断用于指导测量与动态模型的扩展；核心任务始终是统计结构推断与风险预测。

Parameter recovery and observational equivalence参数恢复与观测等价

At fixed N and price scale, finite-ER inversion and joint radial-basis regression recover unseen connectivity with mean absolute errors of 0.0271 and 0.0269 across ten studies. A connected chain and star have the same complete price–volume law.在固定N与价格尺度下，有限ER反演和联合径向基回归在十次研究中恢复未见连接参数的平均绝对误差分别为0.0271和0.0269。连通链与星形图则具有相同的完整量价分布。
