市场风险却不同？

We study how shared trading decisions change market fluctuations, what price and volume can reveal about that structure, and when it helps estimate risk.我们研究共同交易怎样改变市场波动，量价数据能识别其中哪些统计结构，以及这些结构什么时候有助于估计风险。

Each dot represents a trader. Switch to ten groups and watch the dots join and model risk change.每个点是一名交易者。切换到十个群组，观察点怎样聚集、连线，以及模型风险怎样变化。

In the model, grouping changes the variance. In market tests, the mechanism has yet to establish a robust forecasting gain.模型中，分组改变波动。在真实市场检验中，机制的稳健预测增益尚未确立。

Follow the argument沿论证读研究

Each step names the question, the method or observations used, and the evidence to read next.每一步写明研究问题和用到的方法或数据，点进去看相应证据。

  1. 01

    Why can similar volume carry different risk?为什么成交量相近，风险却不同？

    Exact moments of shared group actions and finite random graphs show how group sizes enter aggregate fluctuations.用群体共同动作的精确矩和有限随机图，推导群体规模如何进入宏观波动。

  2. 02

    What can market observations identify?宏观观测能识别什么？

    Use returns, volume, counts and signed flow to test moment inversion and identification limits. Fund disclosures check how overlap and signed flows shape demand.用收益、成交量、笔数和净主动流检验矩反演及识别边界，再用基金披露核对持仓重合与带方向的资金流。

  3. 03

    How does pressure reach prices?交易压力怎样传到价格？

    Vary capacity in paired network scenarios, then align real executions, visible depth and midquotes on one exchange.用配对网络情景改变承接条件，再用同一交易所的真实成交、可见深度和中间价核对价格响应。

  4. 04

    Does the inferred state improve forecasts?估计出的状态能改善预测吗？

    Compare risk forecasts at matched input budgets. Benchmark the mechanism against HAR and check fund-flow states against later disclosures.按可比的输入预算对照机制与 HAR，并用后来公开的真实流量核对预测时估计的基金状态。

  5. 05

    Where can the mechanism be used?这些机制可以用在哪里？

    Use covariance, factor scenarios and market replay to diagnose common exposure, concentration and observed pressure.用协方差、因子情景和真实行情复盘，解释共同暴露、资金集中与交易压力。

In the herding model, group-size moments connect coordination to aggregate price–volume fluctuations.在羊群模型中，群体规模矩将协调结构与宏观量价波动联系起来。

Read the formula with the grouping fixed. N counts traders, q is the chance a group trades, λ scales the price response, and s is each group’s size. S₂ adds the squared sizes.先固定分组 H，再读这个式子。N 是总人数，q 是一组采取交易动作的概率，λ 是价格响应尺度，s 是各组人数。S₂ 把各组人数先平方，再相加。

From group actions to a fast–slow network从群体动作到快慢网络

A weighted, interdependent decision network connects the microscopic mechanism to future joint flow and return scenarios.加权、相互依赖的决策网络将微观机理连接到未来交易流与收益的联合情景。

Controlled interventions connect shared inputs and price feedback to changing alignment and risk. Observable-only estimation and market diagnostics then test how much of that mechanism survives the inverse problem.受控干预将共同输入和价格反馈连接到一致性及风险变化；仅可见历史的估计与市场诊断继续检验，逆问题能够保留多少机理信息。

Parameter recovery and observational equivalence参数恢复与观测等价

At fixed N and price scale, finite-ER inversion and joint radial-basis regression recover unseen connectivity with mean absolute errors of 0.0271 and 0.0269 across ten studies. A connected chain and star have the same complete price–volume law.在固定N与价格尺度下，有限ER反演和联合径向基回归在十次研究中恢复未见连接参数的平均绝对误差分别为0.0271和0.0269。连通链与星形图则具有相同的完整量价分布。

Application to common factors and portfolios应用于共同因子与组合风险

Factor count is not effective diversification.因子数量不等于有效分散。

Extending our herding network, the saved scenarios show how common signals and weak accommodation can offset diversification. Performance-based allocation concentrates weights, but its added risk is not established. An independent historical factor study shows why signed covariance can either amplify or hedge risk.在原有羊群网络中，保存情景展示共同信号与薄弱承接如何抵消分散效果。追逐收益的配置使权重集中，但其风险增量尚未确立。独立的历史因子研究说明，有符号协方差既可能放大风险，也可能对冲风险。
