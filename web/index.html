Why can similar trading volume carry different market risk?为什么成交量相近，
市场风险却不同？

We use prices and trades to study how people acting together change market risk.我们用价格和成交数据研究，人们一起交易时，市场风险会怎样变。

Volume is how much trades. A return is the percentage change in price. Return variance tells us how spread out those changes are.成交量是成交了多少。收益率是价格相对起点变动的比例。这里用收益方差衡量价格变动的幅度有多分散。

In the model, when traders act together, prices can swing more even if the same amount trades on average. In our Bitcoin tests, the model did not improve on the forecast based on past price swings.同向交易会叠加，即使平均成交量一样，模型里的价格也可能起伏更大。在我们的 Bitcoin 对照里，用这个模型预测风险，没能超过只看过去价格波动的基准。

Read the formula with the grouping fixed. N counts traders, q is the chance a group trades, λ scales the price response, and s is each group’s size. S₂ adds the squared sizes.先固定分组 H，再读这个式子。N 是总人数，q 是一组采取交易动作的概率，λ 是价格响应尺度，s 是各组人数。S₂ 把各组人数先平方，再相加。

From group actions to a fast–slow network从群体动作到快慢网络

A weighted, interdependent decision network connects the microscopic mechanism to future joint flow and return scenarios.加权、相互依赖的决策网络将微观机理连接到未来交易流与收益的联合情景。

Controlled interventions connect shared inputs and price feedback to changing alignment and risk. Observable-only estimation and market diagnostics then test how much of that mechanism survives the inverse problem.受控干预将共同输入和价格反馈连接到一致性及风险变化；仅可见历史的估计与市场诊断继续检验，逆问题能够保留多少机理信息。

Parameter recovery and observational equivalence参数恢复与观测等价

At fixed N and price scale, finite-ER inversion and joint radial-basis regression recover unseen connectivity with mean absolute errors of 0.0271 and 0.0269 across ten studies. A connected chain and star have the same complete price–volume law.在固定N与价格尺度下，有限ER反演和联合径向基回归在十次研究中恢复未见连接参数的平均绝对误差分别为0.0271和0.0269。连通链与星形图则具有相同的完整量价分布。

Statistical benchmark on 183 dates独立的 183 日统计基准

The full forecast had 31.20% less error than reusing yesterday’s risk. It had 2.26% less error than a baseline that combines past risk over several time spans, with a range that crosses zero.完整预测比沿用昨天的风险少了 31.20% 的误差。但比结合多个时段历史波动的基准只少了 2.26%，而且这个差值的估计区间跨过零。

Application to common factors and portfolios应用于共同因子与组合风险

Many strategies can still move together策略多了，仍然可能一起涨跌

In the model, common signals and a thin market can undo the benefit of spreading trades across strategies. Moving money toward recent winners concentrates positions. Its effect on risk remains uncertain. Historical strategy returns show that shared moves can add to or offset one another.模型里，不同策略如果听的是同一个信号，或者市场接不住它们同时下的单，分散的好处就会变小。资金追向近期表现好的策略会让仓位集中，它是否进一步推高风险仍不确定。真实历史数据也显示，策略之间的涨跌可以叠加，也可以抵消。
