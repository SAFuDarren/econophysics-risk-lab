Recoverable parameters.
Unrecoverable internal edges.可以恢复的参数，
无法辨认的内部连边。

Price and volume constrain activity and group-size moments. Under an ER family, those moments support conditional connectivity inference. Internal links can still disappear from the observation law.价格和成交量约束活动率与群体规模矩。在ER家族内，这些矩能够支持有条件的连接参数反演；内部连边仍可能从观测分布中消失。
