Herding, price–volume inference and risk羊群机制与量价推断：
市场风险的建模与检验

We study how coordinated trading shapes market fluctuations and what aggregate price and volume can reveal about the underlying network.我们研究共同交易行为如何形成市场波动，以及聚合后的价格与成交量能够揭示哪些网络信息。

Methods combine exact moments, finite random graphs and controlled simulations with Bitcoin and sector-ETF tests.方法结合精确矩关系、有限随机图与受控模拟，并用比特币和行业ETF数据检验。

For known population and order size, price–volume moments recover finite-ER parameters; distinct connected graphs can share the same observation law.在人数与单位订单规模已知时，量价矩可以恢复有限ER参数；不同连通图仍可能共享相同的完整观测分布。

One question from mechanism to evidence从机制到证据，围绕同一个问题

Can a small observable description of collective trading support useful risk estimates? Each step below has a distinct evidential job.能否用少量可观测量描述集体交易，并据此得到有用的风险估计？下面每一步承担不同的证据任务。

  1. Mechanism机制

    Shared directional decisions change weighted flow moments under explicit assumptions.共同方向决策在明确假设下改变加权成交流的矩。

    Conditional mathematical result条件数学结果

  2. Measurement测量与反演

    Ask what aggregate data identify. Execution amplification is an effective statistic; different microstructures can look the same.先问宏观数据能识别什么。执行放大是有效统计量，不同微观结构仍可能观测等价。

    Assumptions and information counted注明假设与信息成本

  3. Market test市场检验

    Negative Δ rejects the original fixed mechanism as a complete market description. Weighted and dynamic extensions remain testable hypotheses.负Δ排除原固定机制作为完整市场描述；加权与动态扩展仍是待检验假设。

    Original restriction retained保留原限制与反证

  4. Risk and use风险与应用

    Map flow to risk, then compare under matched information. Current Bitcoin tests show no extra accuracy or information saving.将成交流映射到风险，再按相同信息比较；目前Bitcoin检验未显示额外精度或信息节省。

    Prediction advantage not established预测优势尚未建立

The historical replay follows this line: observe executions, inspect a model-conditional amplification index, then compare the mapped risk scale with realized fluctuation. Its richer execution data make it an explanatory exercise rather than proof of macro-only risk forecasting.真实回放沿这条线展开：观察成交，检查模型条件下的放大指标，再对照风险映射与已经发生的波动。复盘使用更丰富的成交数据，承担机理讲解功能，不作为仅用宏观量价预测风险的证明。

Parameter recovery and observational equivalence参数恢复与观测等价

At fixed N and price scale, finite-ER inversion and joint radial-basis regression recover unseen connectivity with mean absolute errors of 0.0271 and 0.0269 across ten studies. A connected chain and star have the same complete price–volume law.在固定N与价格尺度下，有限ER反演和联合径向基回归在十次研究中恢复未见连接参数的平均绝对误差分别为0.0271和0.0269。连通链与星形图则具有相同的完整量价分布。
