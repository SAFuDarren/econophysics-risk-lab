    What the signal measures提示来自什么

    Large executions are aggregate trades of at least 100,000 USDT. A signal appears after three completed minutes of unusually one-sided large executions, compared with the preceding two hours.大额成交指单笔不少于10万USDT的聚合成交。连续3个已完成分钟的大额主动买卖明显偏向一侧、并超过此前两小时的阈值时，才会出现提示。

    These excerpts were selected after observing a later move. Timing, pullbacks and competing signals still matter. The signals describe executed buying or selling, not identified accounts or known future orders.这些片段按后续出现明显波动筛选，入场时机、回撤和相反信号仍会影响成绩。提示记录已成交的主动买卖，不代表已识别某个账户或知道其未来订单。

    How the round works交易与评分规则

    You begin with 100,000 virtual units. Opening gross exposure is capped at 1× equity; later price moves can change this ratio. A position closes automatically if equity falls to 25% of its gross exposure. Each fill includes a 5 bp fee and 2 bp slippage. Remaining positions close, with costs, on the final tick.初始资金为 100,000 个虚拟单位，开仓总敞口上限为净值的 1 倍；后续价格变化可能改变这一比例。净值降至总敞口的 25% 时自动平仓。每次成交计入 5 bp 费用与 2 bp 滑点，剩余持仓在最后一步按规则平仓。
