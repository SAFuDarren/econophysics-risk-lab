When does coordination
become market risk?协同在什么环境下
转化为市场风险？

Follow one research chain: macro price–volume observations → effective trading and accommodation states → future risk. First isolate the mechanism, then ask what the observations let us predict.沿同一条研究主线：宏观量价观测 → 有效交易与承接状态 → 未来风险。先隔离机制，再检验观测信息能支持什么预测。

Capacity changes the transmission of coordination.承接容量改变协同向风险的传导。

At κ = .15, the ρc = .65 versus 0 risk increments are 3.306, 2.230 and 1.639 ×10⁻⁶ at L = .5, 1 and 1.5. These equal 7.44%, 6.42% and 5.42% of the common-signal risk, respectively. Weaker accommodation amplifies this paired effect in these fixed scenarios. The covariance–feedback interaction at L = 1 is subadditive; the effects cannot be summed as separate risk shares.在 κ=.15 时，ρc=.65 相对 0，在 L=.5、1、1.5 的风险增量分别为 3.306、2.230、1.639 ×10⁻⁶，相当于各自共同信号情景风险的 7.44%、6.42%、5.42%。这些固定情景中，承接较弱会放大配对效应。L=1 的协方差与反馈交互为次可加，不能把两个效应直接相加成风险份额。

Read the parameters precisely.这些参数分别表示什么？

ρc
Cross-agent correlation of future residual innovations; not the fraction of coordinated traders or ER mean degree.未来残差创新的跨主体相关；不是协同人数比例，也不是 ER 平均度。
κ
Feedback from the last actual normalized calendar return into trading decisions.上一根实际归一化日历收益对交易决策的反馈。
L
Normalized latent accommodation capacity in the model; not observed dollar order-book depth.模型中归一化的隐藏承接容量；不是实测美元订单簿深度。
A separate experiment: removing predictable price pressure另一个固定实验：扣除可预期的价格压力

Here ρc = .65, κ = .15 and L = 1 stay fixed. Only χ, the predictable-impact subtraction in the price equation, changes. This control is independent of the capacity selector above.此处固定 ρc=.65、κ=.15、L=1。只改变价格方程中的可预期影响扣除 χ，与上面的容量选择独立。

Partial subtraction reduces risk relative to none. The .5 → 1 contrast has an interval crossing zero: it establishes neither further benefit nor an optimum. The two additional recovery cells are not part of the 27-cell map.部分扣除相对不扣除减少风险；.5→1 的差值区间跨零，未建立进一步收益或最优扣除程度。两个额外实验格不属于 27 格主图。

Simulated estimates · Precision and observability checked separately模拟估计 · 分开检验精度与可观测性

A clearer effect is not automatically a better forecast.效应估计更清楚，不等于预测更准确。

Termwise conditional integration reduces simulation noise while preserving feedback from actual random returns. Then we test whether state differences add information beyond the activity already observed.逐项条件积分降低模拟噪声，同时保留实际随机收益对后续决策的反馈。然后检验状态差异是否在已知活动信息之外增加预测信息。

Some noise is removable.部分噪声可以减少。

This is an empirical variance reduction for this estimator, not a general variance theorem. The earlier fitted-parameter Monte Carlo error remains unresolved.这是本估计器的实测方差降低，不是一般方差定理；此前拟合参数的蒙特卡洛误差仍未解决。

Mean effects and predictive increments differ.平均效应与预测增量不同。

An intervention compares different worlds. A forecast must distinguish high-risk and low-risk states within the observed world. Existing activity predictors can absorb a mean uplift, leaving no incremental QLIKE gain from even precisely estimated ΔC or ΔF.干预比较不同世界；预测要区分同一观测世界里的高风险与低风险状态。已有活动预测器可以吸收平均抬升，因此即使 ΔC、ΔF 估计更准确，也可能没有额外 QLIKE 收益。

Environment is the sharper open question.环境状态是更明确的缺口。

Adding the hidden environment improves this simulated task; extra direction, memory and residual summaries do not add clear gain. The joint improvement cannot be assigned to activity alone. A fitted-model null is not a proof that macro inference is impossible.加入隐藏环境能改善本模拟任务；再加方向、记忆与残差摘要没有明确增益。联合改善不能只归因于活动。某个拟合器没有增益，不证明宏观反演不可能。

How are the two price channels separated?两个价格通道如何区分？

With return r = μ + σgε and conditionally independent, zero-mean, unit-variance price noise, expected future squared-return risk satisfies:当收益 r=μ+σgε，且定价噪声条件独立、零均值、方差为 1 时，未来平方收益风险的期望满足：

E[R] = E[Σ μ²] + E[Σ σ²g²]

The accumulation uses the conditional second moment while the dynamics still use actual random returns. μ includes drift and transmitted price pressure. Neither component is an identified coordination share in Bitcoin.累计风险时使用条件二阶矩，动力学仍使用实际随机收益。μ 包含漂移与传导价格压力。两个分量都不是已识别的 Bitcoin 协同风险份额。

Precision: 256 new origins × 512 futures per arm.精度实验：256 个新起点，每分支 512 条未来。

QLIKE measures risk-mean forecast loss; lower is better. Coverage is for a 90% interval and must be read with width and independent validation. Post-run controls were not used to reselect the primary model.QLIKE 衡量风险均值预测误差，越低越好。覆盖率对应 90% 区间，需结合宽度与独立验证。事后对照未用于重新选择主模型。

An identity sharpens the question.一个恒等式澄清了归因。

Only with χ = 1, constant local noise, no activity forecast and no fallback, the local risk sum satisfies:只有在 χ=1、固定局部噪声、关闭活动预测且不使用回退项时，局部风险总和满足：

Rlocal = 12 [ α² + mean20((r − α)²) ]

This restricted local total becomes a past return second moment. The selected full component model still uses activity forecasts and relative components. χ = 1 is a selected closure form, not a measured claim that Bitcoin perfectly anticipates pressure.这个受限局部总和退化为历史收益二阶矩。选定的完整组件模型仍含活动预测与相对分量。χ=1 是有限候选中选出的闭合形式，不是 Bitcoin 完全预期交易压力的实测结论。

What this research adds这一轮的实际贡献

The mechanism explains when the same activity becomes more dangerous: correlation, actual-return feedback and accommodation interact. Precision experiments separate simulation noise from reproducible state variation. Bitcoin controls then identify the unresolved step: extracting an environment state that adds risk information beyond simple observable moments. Mean amplification is established in these model scenarios; unique market forecasting superiority is not.机理说明相同活动何时更危险：相关、实际收益反馈与承接环境共同作用。精度实验区分模拟噪声与可重复的状态差异。Bitcoin 对照定位未完成的一步：提取比简单可见矩增加更多风险信息的环境状态。这些模型情景中的平均放大已得到验证；独特的市场预测优势尚未建立。
