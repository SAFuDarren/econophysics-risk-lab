Read the factor controls先读懂因子与规模

A factor is a signal several strategies can follow. A scales order weights, M counts signal groups, c sets how much of the signal is shared, and L controls accommodation. Different group labels can still represent the same signal.因子是多种策略可以共同响应的信号。A 放大订单权重，M 是信号组数，c 表示各组共享多少信号，L 表示承接能力。即使分成几组，共用同一信号时仍可能一起交易。

What is held fixed?哪些条件保持不变？

The original 48-unit fast/slow network, marginal signal variance, activity draws and price parameters are retained. At fixed A, factor and capacity comparisons have identical volume, count and pricing-noise paths. Actors remain network-dependent even when their factor innovations are independent.保留原48单元快慢网络、单体信号方差、活动抽样和价格参数。在相同 A 下，因子与容量对照的成交量、笔数和定价噪声路径相同。即使因子创新独立，主体仍通过网络相互影响。

have different net effects.不同反馈通道，

The first comparison removes one channel at A = 1, M = 12 independent inputs and L = 1.3. The volume-matched comparison uses A = 2. These effects have distinct references and cannot be added into risk shares.第一组在 A=1、12个独立因子输入、L=1.3 时逐项关闭通道；成交量匹配比较使用 A=2。各效应参考情景不同，不能相加成风险份额。

Channel removed − full network关闭通道 − 完整网络

Directional scale beyond activity成交活动之外的方向规模

Full directional scaling increases expected risk by 17.23% relative to the matched-volume control. Volume, counts and pricing noise match path by path.相对成交量匹配控制，扩大完整方向流使预期风险增加17.23%。两者成交量、笔数和定价噪声逐路径相同。

A network is not a single amplification switch.网络不是单一的放大开关。

Removing peer influence lowers risk by 4.73%; removing actual-return feedback lowers it by 5.71%. Removing same-unit fast/slow coupling instead raises risk by 7.14%. These are net effects through the retained dynamics and price-recovery law in this configuration.关闭同伴影响使风险下降4.73%，关闭实际收益反馈使风险下降5.71%；关闭同一主体的快慢层耦合反而使风险上升7.14%。这是当前配置经完整动态和价格恢复规律得到的净作用。

A separate price-risk test单独检验价格风险

Past performance changes weights.过去表现改变权重。

Terminal HHI rises by 1.78% and 4.21% for 4 and 12 groups versus fixed weights. Window-mean pre-bar HHI rises by 0.99% and 2.27% descriptively. Terminal HHI includes the final update for the next, untraded bar; it is not a cause assigned to the already completed window.相对固定权重，4组和12组的终值HHI分别提高1.78%和4.21%；窗口平均bar前HHI描述性增加0.99%和2.27%。终值HHI包含最后一次对下一根尚未交易bar的更新，不能将它倒置为已结束窗口风险的原因。

Concentration is not a risk forecast.集中度不是直接的风险预测。

All four primary price-risk intervals cross zero. This does not establish harmless concentration or long-run stability. The score-identity permutation retains its own completed returns. Real fund wealth, margin, financing and immediate rebalance orders are outside this experiment.四项价格风险主比较的区间均跨零。这不证明集中没有风险或长期稳定。评分身份置换仍保留本臂已完成收益。真实基金净值、保证金、融资和即时再平衡订单不在本实验内。
